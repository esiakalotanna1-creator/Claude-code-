# House rules for every video in this repo

These rules apply to every video made here, with any engine (toon-reel, what-if or something new). They were set by
the owner of the channel.

1. **Intro brand.** Every video opens on an old-television overlay with static noise for **under 1 second**. It's
   quick, and the static blends into the first shot instead of hard-cutting. Tool: `tools/tv_intro.py`.
2. **Fast audio.** Voices are fast and tight. No breathers, no dead air between lines, and no slow dramatic pauses.
   Use Kokoro at speed ~1.15+ (or pitch/speed up), gaps of 0.05–0.1 s, and trimmed takes.
3. **Main-character costume brand.** Main characters always wear all black:
   - boys and men: a black shirt and black trousers;
   - girls and women: a black shirt and a black skirt;
   - every one has **"Neat"** printed across the back of the black shirt (rig look key `back_text="Neat"`).

Also keep the owner's usage limit in mind: avoid unnecessary test renders and send results when done.
