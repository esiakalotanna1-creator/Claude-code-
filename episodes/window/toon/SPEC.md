# The Man at the Window: the shot-by-shot brief

50 s, serious horror throughout, clear visual reveal. Narrator: a low, measured thriller voice with tonal direction.

Layout (fixed, every shot): garden + father, then window glass, then stranger + living room. The stranger's forehead and palm touch the INSIDE of the glass. The father never enters. Nobody changes sides.

Setting: quiet suburban house, late afternoon. Outside: clipped hedges, scattered leaves, a narrow garden bed under the window, warm sunlight across the lawn, brick wall. Inside: dim living room, armchair, floor lamp, staircase up. Stranger: natural contours, slightly yellowish skin, tangled hair, broad red-lipped smile; no monster teeth or glowing eyes. Soft daylight on the father, reflected daylight on the stranger; the light never changes for the reveal.
