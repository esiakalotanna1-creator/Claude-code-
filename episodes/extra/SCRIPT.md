# ONE EXTRA: script (3 parts)

Dark-humor horror for TikTok in three parts of about 80–90 s. Each part opens on its most important line with a
striking image in the first two seconds, and ends on a cliffhanger.

**Narrator:** TOBI, 13. Dry, funny, counts everything when he's nervous.
**Family (five people, and this number matters):** MOM, DAD, NIA (16, sarcastic, always on her phone), TOBI, and BENJI
(6, the little brother, quiet, always in the background drawing, and the only one who remembers Tobi's birthday).

**The rules of this world** (the viewer learns them one at a time):
1. Every night at midnight, one extra person appears in every home on Earth.
2. If you were asleep at midnight, you wake up remembering them as family. If you were awake, you know.
3. Never let an Extra find out that you know.
4. When the Extras outnumber the real family, the real ones start to fade: first from the photos, then from memory.
5. At midnight you can count your family out loud. Anyone you leave out of the count has to leave. If you leave out
   someone real, they are gone for good.

---

## PART 1: "SET FOR SIX" (about 80 s)

**HOOK (0–3 s).** A family dinner seen from above. Six plates. Five people eat normally. The sixth, a smiling man
nobody has introduced, slowly turns his head and looks straight into the camera.
Card: **ONE EXTRA · PART 1**

> **TOBI (V.O.):** "There are five people in my family. Tonight, my mom set the table for six."

**(3–10 s).** A globe at night; one new light blinks on in every house. A counter: **WORLD POPULATION +2,100,000,000**.
> "It happened in every home on Earth, at midnight. And almost nobody noticed. Because if you were asleep… you woke up
> loving them."

**(10–22 s): dark humor.** The stranger is "Uncle Dave". He fixes the sink. He laughs at Dad's jokes.
> **TOBI:** "Mom. Who is that?"
> **MOM:** "That's your Uncle Dave! He taught you to ride a bike."
> **TOBI:** "I can't ride a bike."
> **MOM:** "…And whose fault is that?"
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "I only knew because I was awake at midnight. So was my sister. She was on her phone. For once, that
> saved her life."

**(22–34 s).** Nia and Tobi whisper in the hallway. Behind them, Benji tapes a drawing to the fridge: five stick
figures. Uncle Dave appears at the end of the hall, smiling, holding a wrench.
> **NIA:** "Rule one. We never let them know that we know."
> **UNCLE DAVE:** "Know what, champ?"
> **TOBI:** "…That you're my favourite uncle."
> **UNCLE DAVE (pleased):** "I'm your only uncle." (beat) "So far."

**(34–46 s).** Benji hands Tobi a homemade card, early.
> **BENJI:** "Your birthday is in forty-one days. I'm counting."
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "Benji is six. He's the only person in this house who has ever remembered my birthday."

Midnight. The clock flips. Day two: seven plates. A cheerful "cousin" who hugs everyone too long. Day three: eight.
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "They were polite. They did chores. They were, honestly, better at being a family than we were. That
> was the scary part."

**(46–62 s).** Night. Tobi and Nia dig out the old family photo album to get proof. They flip the pages: Uncle Dave is
in every photo now. At the beach. At the wedding. Holding baby Nia.
> **NIA:** "They're in all of them. They go all the way back."

**(62–80 s) CLIFFHANGER.** Nia turns to the first page: the hospital, Mom holding a newborn. She flips forward.
Birthdays. Holidays. School photos. Her finger stops. She looks up at Tobi, slowly.
> **NIA:** "Tobi… you're not in any of these."

Close on Tobi's face. Behind him, in the dark doorway, three Extras stand smiling.
> **NIA (whisper):** "How long have you been my brother?"

Card: **IS HE REAL? · FOLLOW FOR PART 2**

---

## PART 2: "MAJORITY RULES" (about 85 s)

**HOOK (0–3 s).** A framed family photo on the wall. Tobi's face in it is **fading out** while we watch, like breath
off a window.
Card: **ONE EXTRA · PART 2**

> **TOBI (V.O.):** "My sister found my baby photos. I wasn't in them. Then I watched myself disappear from the one on
> the wall."

**(3–14 s).** Tobi runs to the kitchen doorframe: the pencil height marks. "TOBI age 7", "TOBI age 9". The pencil lines
are thinning out, letter by letter.
> "I wasn't one of them. It was worse. I was being replaced. Rule four: when they outnumber you, the real ones start to
> fade."

A head count in the living room, with numbers popping up over each head: **5 real. 6 Extras.**

**(14–28 s): dark humor.** Dinner. The Extras hold a vote.
> **UNCLE DAVE:** "All in favour of fish?" (six hands go up) "Democracy!"
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "I hate fish. I've never lost an election this badly."

**(28–42 s).** Morning. Dad's chair is empty. His mug is gone from the shelf. In the wall photo there is a gap where he
stood.
> **TOBI:** "Mom, where's Dad?"
> **MOM (smiling, puzzled):** "Who, sweetie?"
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "My dad was gone. He owed me twenty dollars."

Benji quietly adds something to his fridge drawing: he crosses nobody out. He draws Dad again, bigger.

**(42–58 s).** Nia finds a video from a kid across the world, filmed under a blanket, whispering.
> **KID ON PHONE:** "At midnight, count your family out loud. Point at each one. Whoever you leave out has to go. But if
> you leave out someone real…" (the video cuts off as a smiling face lifts the blanket)
> **NIA:** "So we count. Tonight."
> **TOBI:** "And if we get one wrong?"
> **NIA:** "Then don't get one wrong."

**(58–72 s).** 11:58 p.m. Everyone is at the table: eleven people, all smiling, all looking the same in the low light.
Nia leans in to whisper, and stops. Her eyes go blank.
> **NIA:** "…What's my middle name? Tobi. I can't remember my middle name."

**(72–85 s) CLIFFHANGER.** 11:59. Mom turns to Tobi, warm, a little confused.
> **MOM:** "Sweetie… which one are you again?"

The clock's second hand climbs. Every Extra at the table turns to Tobi at the same moment, still smiling.
Split screen: **A: Count now** and risk erasing someone real. **B: Stay quiet** and be forgotten by morning.
Card: **A OR B? WHAT DO YOU DO? · FOLLOW FOR PART 3**

---

## PART 3: "COUNT TO FIVE" (about 90 s)

**HOOK (0–3 s).** The clock hits **12:00**. Tobi is standing on his chair, arm out, pointing. Eleven faces stare up at
him.
Card: **ONE EXTRA · FINAL PART**

> **TOBI (V.O.):** "The rule is simple. Count your family out loud, and everyone else has to leave. Get one wrong, and
> you erase someone you love."

**(3–16 s).** He freezes. They all look real. Then he remembers something.
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "The Extras knew everything about us. Every memory. Every date. They were perfect. And that was the
> trick. Real families aren't."
> **TOBI (out loud):** "Quick question. When's my birthday?"

**(16–30 s): dark humor.** Six Extras answer instantly, in unison, with the exact date. Mom squints.
> **MOM:** "It's… March. The fourteenth? Fifteenth. It's definitely a number."
> **NIA:** "Summer?"
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "That's my mother. Only a real mother gets it that wrong."
> **TOBI (pointing):** "One. Two."

**(30–46 s).** He counts fast. Mom. Nia. Himself. He says Dad's name at the empty chair, and Dad flickers back in
mid-sip of coffee.
> **DAD:** "Did I miss something?"
> **TOBI:** "You owe me twenty dollars. Four."

One seat left to count. The Extras lean forward, smiling wider.

**(46–60 s).** The uncounted Extras stand up, one by one, and walk politely to the front door.
> **UNCLE DAVE (at the door):** "That sink's going to leak again in a week, champ."
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "I almost missed him. Almost."

The door closes. The wall photo fills back in. The pencil marks on the doorframe darken. The family collapses into a
hug.

**(60–74 s).** Morning. Sun. The news: world population back to normal. Mom sets the table, humming. Tobi counts the
plates out of habit.
> **TOBI (V.O.):** "One. Two. Three. Four…" (beat) "Five."
> **TOBI:** "Mom. Why are there five plates?"
> **MOM (laughs):** "Habit, I guess. There's four of us. There's always been four of us."

**(74–90 s) FINAL TWIST.** Tobi stops at the fridge. A child's drawing: five stick figures. The smallest one is holding
a card. On Tobi's desk upstairs, a homemade birthday card: *"41 days. I'm counting."* He doesn't know who made it.

Flash back to midnight, the moment he asked the question. Seven voices answered with the exact date, not six. One of
them was small, and came from the end of the table.

> **TOBI (V.O.):** "The Extras knew my birthday perfectly. So I didn't count anyone who did."
> (beat) "There are four people in my family. There have always been four."
> (beat) "So why do I keep setting the table for five?"

Last image: the wall photo. Four people smiling. And a small gap at the front, exactly the height of a six-year-old.
Card: **ONE EXTRA · THE END**

## Why the openings work

| Part | First line (in the first 2–3 s) | First image | What it promises |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "There are five people in my family. Tonight, my mom set the table for six." | A stranger at dinner turns and looks into the camera | One simple, wrong detail the viewer has to see explained |
| 2 | "My sister found my baby photos. I wasn't in them." | His face fading out of the family photo | Answers part 1's question with a worse one |
| 3 | "Count your family out loud, and everyone else has to leave. Get one wrong, and you erase someone you love." | A boy on a chair pointing at eleven smiling faces at midnight | States the stakes of the A/B choice in one line |

Alternative first lines to test:
- P1: "Count the people in your house right now. Tomorrow, there will be one more."
- P1: "My mom says Uncle Dave taught me to ride a bike. I don't have an Uncle Dave. I can't ride a bike."
- P2: "When they outnumber you, they hold a vote. We lost. Then my dad disappeared."
- P3: "I asked eleven people when my birthday was. The ones who got it right had to leave."

## How the ending is planted
- Part 1's first line says **five** people. The last line says **four**.
- Benji is in the background of parts 1 and 2 and almost never speaks. Viewers who rewatch will spot him.
- Part 1 tells us Benji is the only one who remembers Tobi's birthday. In part 3, that is exactly what gets him erased.
- Benji's fridge drawing appears in all three parts.

## Production notes (for when we animate)
- Characters: Tobi, Nia, Mom, Dad, Benji, Uncle Dave, plus 5 more Extras (friendly faces, smiles a little too wide).
- Sets: dining room (the main set, seen from above and from table height), kitchen with fridge and doorframe, hallway
  with the wall photo, Tobi's and Nia's room for the album and phone scenes. Plus the globe insert.
- New effects needed: a face fading out of a framed photo, pencil marks fading, head-count numbers over heads, a person
  flickering back into a chair, the A/B split screen.
